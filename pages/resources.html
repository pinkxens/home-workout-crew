Body measurement template

Optional, and only if you find it motivating: some people like tracking measurements alongside how workouts feel, since the scale doesn't show strength or mobility gains. Measure every two to four weeks, at the same time of day, with the tape snug but not tight. This is one way to track progress, not a requirement.
